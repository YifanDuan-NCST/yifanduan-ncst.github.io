<div class="lang-en" markdown="1">
I am currently pursuing my doctoral degree majoring in Metallurgical Engineering at [North China University of Science and Technology](https://www.ncst.edu.cn/) <img src='../images/NCST.svg' style="width: 6em;">. I belong to the Low-Carbon & Intelligent Metallurgy Big Data Team and the Yanzhao Iron and Steel Laboratory. Feel free to contact me via [yifanduan@stu.ncst.edu.cn](mailto:yifanduan@stu.ncst.edu.cn) for any forms of academic collaboration. We are recruiting master’s and PhD candidates to join our research group.

I completed my undergraduate study in Metallurgical Engineering at [North China University of Science and Technology](https://www.ncst.edu.cn/). I obtained the postgraduate recommendation qualification without taking the national postgraduate entrance examination in 2022 ([qualification notice](http://www.ysygaokao.cn/ceeInfocxDetails.aspx?Id=2441)). In 2023, I continued my PhD study in Metallurgical Engineering at [North China University of Science and Technology](https://www.ncst.edu.cn/) under the supervision of Professor [Ran Liu](https://yjxy.ncst.edu.cn/col/1423045905867/2016/03/23/1458697698683.html). Our research team maintains close industrial cooperation with [HBIS Group](https://www.hbisco.com/) Tangshan Iron and Steel Company, Chengde Iron and Steel Company and other iron and steel enterprises.

In 2025, I was awarded the National Scholarship for Doctoral Students (Award No. BSY202514105) ([official portal](https://zwfw.moe.gov.cn/xszz/)) and selected for the 2025 Doctoral Program of the China Association for Science and Technology Youth Science and Technology Talent Cultivation Project ([NCST announcement](https://newstest.ncst.edu.cn/col/1393558054724/2026/01/08/1767841936525.html)), being the sole recipient from North China University of Science and Technology in the first batch. In addition, my personally verified CSDN account, [Yifan Duan - NCST](https://blog.csdn.net/IT_XiaoFan_?spm=1000.2115.3001.5343), has accumulated over 130,000 page views and keeps growing.

My research interests cover blast furnace ironmaking, artificial intelligence, domain-specific large language models and industrial AI agents. My research focuses on data-driven intelligent applications throughout the whole ironmaking process. Since enrollment, I have filed 4 national invention patents. As the first author, I have published 10 academic papers, including 1 paper in the CAS Q1 TOP journal, 1 paper in CAS Q2 journal, 1 paper in CAS Q3 journal, and 7 papers in Chinese Excellent Academic Journals. Two additional manuscripts targeting CAS Q1 journals are currently under external review. I have attended more than 10 domestic and international academic conferences. I delivered oral presentations at the 6th and 7th Youth Scholars Forum on Ironmaking, as well as the 2024 National Annual Conference on Blast Furnace Ironmaking. I preside over 1 Hebei Provincial Postgraduate Innovation Funding Project, participate as a core member in 1 Hebei Provincial Natural Science Foundation project, and take part in 3 industrial horizontal research projects supported by HBIS Tangsteel and Chengsteel. I have won multiple first prizes in the National University Metallurgical Science and Technology Competition.
